# Farming Interest Flow

`FARMING` is a daily job backed by one persisted `PlayerNpcFarmPlan`. The flow is:

1. Satisfy the normal starter-log target.
2. Search outward from the NPC in nearest-first order for a flat 5-8 by 5-8 hoe-compatible dirt site. Width and depth roll independently; larger sites may omit their four corners. A new plan is persisted only when its surface elevation is locally bounded and its entrance has an exact reachable path.
3. Persist the site, one randomized central irrigation cell, the gate facing the anchor, its protected entrance path, and setup phase.
4. Gather the plan's calculated log reserve, then two stone. Basic gear crafting supplies a pickaxe and hoe while farm setup is unfinished.
5. Clear farm surface/headroom obstructions, place the water source, craft/place fences and an open gate, then till every crop cell except irrigation and the entrance path.
6. Repair missing or water-filled ground with carried dirt, restore water/perimeter/gate/tilled ground, and clear later surface or entrance blockers.
7. Harvest mature crops, gather reachable nearby mature village crops, plant wheat/carrot/potato/beetroot, and break reachable grass when seeds are missing. Explore to a new local area only when no actionable nearby supply exists.
8. At night, a FARMING-interest worker whose current-day selected job is either FARMING or absent anchors a passive temporary camp at its current dry position when no valid farm plan exists, using bounded local walk/look/sneak activity until daylight or plan creation. This absent-job fallback is required for NPCs first loaded/spawned after the daytime job-roll window. A current-day selection for another job retains ownership and suppresses the farming camp. With a plan, an eligible pure FARMING worker returns through the saved open gate to an outside-only camp stand. Camp walking also stays outside; the worker can place/use/reclaim its temporary furnace and add up to four deterministic low-light fence-top torches. Existing torches and unsupported fence shapes are skipped.
9. During clear daytime, a fully planted farm may use a bounded low-priority stroll on explicit entrance-path cells or a safe ring around the perimeter. Any actionable owned crop work suppresses or preempts the stroll.

A READY farm does not treat an absent old hoe as work by itself. Replacement-hoe and two-stone support demand return only after an exposed crop-ground cell actually needs tilling; planted cells remain valid until harvest exposes their support. READY farming days also suppress optional general gear crafting, while concrete lighting log/stone support and emergency escape tools remain eligible.

New farming NPCs always receive a water bucket. Most receive carrots or potatoes; some receive wheat seeds. `FillWaterBucketGoal` skips the owned irrigation source and starts only when it finds an exact reachable dry interaction stand beside another source; failed or completed fill episodes stop their navigation so an unreachable source cannot leave a stale route behind.

For NPCs with both BUILDING and FARMING, the strict unfinished home is forced as the daily job until blueprint placement and terraform prep are complete. Farm selection validates against the home, and building-site selection validates against an existing farm.

Ownership safety lives in `PlayerNpcFarmPlan` and `FarmAi`. `FarmAi.isOwnedFarmDestructionProtected(...)` is the authoritative default-deny boundary used by block-breaking/clearing helpers: it protects the owned surface plus the buffered support column down to world minimum. Generic logging, descent, escape, mining, dig-down, terraforming, crafting-table placement, bucket filling, and building selection cannot excavate it. Only explicitly revalidated setup maintenance and mature crop/forage actions opt in.

Ground repair runs before tilling and READY crop work. Air, replaceable blocks, or water flow in an owned ground cell are replaced with one carried hoe-compatible dirt block, except the irrigation cell and any cell with a crop above it. Repairing a crop-ground cell immediately persists `TILL`, so crop work cannot resume until the exact reachable hoe action restores farmland; coarse/rooted material is placed as ordinary dirt to keep this a single till interaction. Healthy entrance-path ground is retained; a missing path support is restored as dirt and explicitly remains untilled. Placement and tilling each use their own exact reachable stand and one successful one-shot hand action. If dirt is absent, the same setup goal searches a bounded nearby surface set and gathers one reachable external dirt/grass block without mining the farm/home footprint.

A correct but closed owned gate is opened before setup scans interior repair/maintenance targets, even if the persisted plan is still `GATE` or `TILL`. Opening it invalidates old clear-target cooldowns recorded under the closed topology. Interior soft-cover clearing stages from the same farmland-capable exact interaction stands as placement, then delegates the actual bounded break to `ClearBlockAi`; it does not require farmland to pass the generic full-solid support test.

Maintenance-clear staging is one bounded episode even when its current eye ray discovers a protected dirt/farm blocker. A failed logical stand is excluded for the rest of that episode, and each replacement must be an exact-reachable farmland-capable stand whose centered eye ray first resolves to an allowed clear target. Stand reselection does not reset the 25-second timer. Exhausting the bounded alternatives cools both the requested and resolved targets and issues the existing short forced-rise handoff, preventing a same-cell `approaching farm work area` loop with completed navigation.

Arrival at those logical farm stands accounts for farmland's 15/16-block surface. The NPC must be grounded and centered on the exact stand X/Z, but may physically be up to the non-full support offset below its logical stand Y; raw floored `blockPosition()` equality is not used. This lets a completed navigation path hand an interior sapling/foliage request from return movement to the authorized farm clear instead of looping route recovery. The protected generic return helper still cannot clear owned soil/crops/fences itself.

The saved entrance lane is intentionally not tilled: `pathPositions()` is removed from `cropGroundPositions()` along with irrigation. Other dirt/grass cells inside `cropGroundPositions()` are real pending till targets, but setup processes safe crop/headroom obstructions such as a sapling before selecting those hoe actions.

Farm lighting reuses `MiningNightCampGoal` and `FurnaceAi`. A ready farm with an unlit owned fence gathers a small three-log reserve; if coal/charcoal is absent, it also gathers eight furnace stone when no furnace is available. Camp smelts one log into charcoal using the existing owned temporary-furnace lifecycle, crafts torches without consuming unfinished fence reserves, places them with normal block-use animation, and reclaims the furnace plus all input/fuel/output when camp ends. Shared temporary-furnace coordinates carry an explicit `cooking` or `night_camp` kind: camp recovery must never adopt or pack a cooking furnace, while an explicit camp furnace remains recoverable even when normal camping is no longer eligible. Kindless legacy coordinates are adopted as camp only while night-camp behavior is currently eligible, avoiding daytime theft of a pre-kind cooking furnace.

Once a fence-light episode selects an owned fence target and stand, it owns navigation until placement or a bounded failure; crossing the outside-camp threshold cannot replace that route with `returning to farm camp`. Repathing is throttled so the navigator can report a real stuck route. A stuck/unreachable stand delegates only its target-local body/path candidates to `ClearBlockAi`, with at most six clear starts and a two-block horizontal fence envelope. The opt-in owned-farm override is revalidated before and after blocker retargeting and accepts only logs, leaves, vines, grass/ferns, dead bush, or snow. Expected fence/gate cells, farm ground/support/path/irrigation/crops, home/build cells, block entities, temporary crafting tables, and temporary pillar supports remain protected. After the light is placed or the bounded episode aborts, ordinary outside-camp return behavior resumes.

Cook and camp furnace placement reject the full farm/work/entrance X/Z exclusion for every NPC that owns a farm, even when Mining or Fishing is the active mixed-interest job. Temporary placement first searches exact reachable stands outside the saved gate. A legacy tracked furnace already inside the exclusion is packed before reuse: its input, fuel, output, and furnace item are snapshotted, slots are emptied before removal to prevent duplicate drops, restored if removal fails, and returned remainder-safely after success.

`CookFoodGoal` placement trace details include the selected pending work reason (`farm torch charcoal`, build glass/stone/torch charcoal, food, or ore), so an otherwise surprising daytime furnace can be attributed without inferring from inventory screenshots.

Crop planting/harvesting/bonemeal arbitration shares `GatherLogsGoal.hasLogSupplyDemand(...)` with the real gather goal, covering both farm-specific needs and the NPC's rolled general log reserve. Actionable work in the ready owned farm is considered first, and a crop action that has started is allowed to finish even if the gather cooldown expires while the farmer is walking to its interaction stand. Empty crop cells rotate through a bounded exact-path search, preventing one unreachable nearest cell from starving the remainder; a failed plant uses a short retry rather than the generic long idle cooldown. Once GatherLogs itself starts, its weak active-episode latch keeps both crop work and seed exploration from preempting the selected tree route. With no active logging episode, empty farmland with no planting item is a continuity requirement: reachable local grass/crops may run through FarmCrop, while the absence of a nearby reachable supply releases ExploreAround. Neither an unstarted log demand nor the crop-action cooldown suppresses that supply work, so the nearby-yield and crop-defer predicates cannot leave a seedless farmer mutually blocked. FarmSetup repair/infrastructure work retains its normal priority, and mixed BUILDING workers keep the existing strict home precedence.

When log gathering needs dirt for a pillar, `GatherLogsGoal` must exclude every block protected by `FarmAi.isOwnedFarmDestructionProtected(...)` while selecting dirt. The shared breaker intentionally rejects owned farm ground; accepting it during selection would repeatedly choose the same unbreakable farm grass and prevent the farmer from reaching the log.

Ready-farm crop work also owns the short six-to-thirteen-tick pause after each successful action. Farming-support stone gathering checks `FarmCropGoal.hasActionableOwnedFarmWork(...)` both before starting and while continuing, so it cannot use that pause to pull a farmer out through the protected stone-work egress between consecutive plant actions. The setup `GATHER_STONE` bootstrap is unchanged because crop work is not actionable until strict READY. A valid tracked temporary furnace also satisfies the farm-lighting furnace availability check; placing/loading that furnace must not immediately recreate an eight-stone furnace demand while charcoal is still smelting.

Seed/crop exploration is allowed to issue the shared bounded exploration climb request. If a seedless farmer is below the surface and no reachable surface exploration target exists, `ExploreAroundGoal` selects a nearby supported surface within its ten-block climb budget, records the seed/crop exploration owner, and hands off to the existing upward escape/pillar path. A failed climb can only hand back to the same exploration mode, which resumes a bounded local fallback walk or the next seed/crop search instead of leaving the farmer idle in the shaft.

Active work and exploration destinations remain owned while the NPC is making water progress. There is no global MOVE-owning water goal: priority-zero `FloatGoal` supplies buoyancy while `PathNavigationAi` passes the requested destination into `WaterEscapeAi`, and direct log/stone/build callers pass their current stand or work target. While submerged, `ExploreAroundGoal` may select a validated dry surface target even though land pathfinding cannot yet reach it, ticks water travel every goal tick between normal repaths, and never converts that water route into an upward pillar request. The helper swims toward the owner destination at speed `<= 1.0D`; a dry exit is accepted only if it advances toward that destination, so it cannot return to the bank the NPC just left. Two seconds without progress may trigger bounded footing placement only in one-block-deep water or beside a solid wall/shore obstruction. An NPC that remains stalled receives three bounded attempts before that destination is marked failed for the current goal episode, after which `ExploreAroundGoal` can select another target. Footing placement rejects owned-farm destruction protection and the home/build footprint.

Farm-support log/stone gathering keeps its active route during a river crossing. A wet body bobbing
one block below the saved home-origin Y is not home-surface recovery, even when its X/Z remains in
the home build footprint; otherwise higher-priority ReturnHome cancels the gathering owner and the
farmer repeatedly rescans the same supply target. Direct gather water steering runs every server
tick and includes capped horizontal acceleration toward the saved work stand; FloatGoal supplies
vertical buoyancy but does not move the NPC across the river by itself.

During that bounded upward escape, a bed intersecting the active pillar/body column may be cleared even inside the home/build footprint. The exception is bed-only: non-bed build blocks remain protected, owned-farm destruction protection is checked for both matching bed halves, and occupied beds are rejected. Vanilla destruction removes the paired half and produces one bed drop; a no-drop fallback only repairs a paired half left behind by the neighbor update. This prevents a farmer standing on a bed from looping indefinitely at `pillaring 0/N shifting pillar base`.

Bone meal targets only reachable, non-mature crops inside the owned plan. Bones are crafted only when such a target exists. The crop is revalidated at use time, growth is committed without a random can-use failure, and inventory is restored if no mutation occurs. Harvesting accepts only `CropBlock.isMaxAge(...)`; subsequent planting consumes a seed/crop item only after the empty farmland target is revalidated.

Any NPC carrying BUILDING interest with a saved home or selected layout defers normal Farming,
Mining, and Fishing outdoor camp. At night/thunder, saved-home return/shelter behavior wins
regardless of that day's selected job. A mixed builder/farmer with neither a home/layout nor a
valid farm plan may use the same passive no-plan farming camp instead of idling. The bootstrap camp
does not place a furnace or mutate terrain, will not start/continue in water, and immediately yields
to combat/healing/emergency goals; plan validity is rechecked on a 20-tick cadence. This gate does
not abandon a previously placed owned camp furnace: its bounded recovery runs first.

`MiningNightCampGoal` is registered as ordinary startup-gated work. The startup grace and UUID
stagger can delay its first activation during world restoration, but they cannot explain a late
idle episode after release. Daily job selection rolls only during daytime ticks 1-11999 and does
not deliberately clear a job at night. A null current-day job after that window is therefore an
unselected farming-night fallback, not evidence that the NPC lacks its FARMING interest.

Stone protection is deeper than ordinary surface ownership. `FarmAi.isBelowOwnedFarmFootprint(...)` protects the farm and a two-block X/Z support buffer from the farm ground down to world minimum. `GatherStoneGoal` moves out of the farm work/entrance footprint using bounded safe egress selection before it scans, clears, or mines; `DigDownForStoneGoal` rejects protected origins and dig/clear targets. Both revalidate blockers after `ClearBlockAi` resolves or retargets them, so mining cannot tunnel under irrigation, crops, fences, or the gate.

All setup placements use a reachable exact stand within the real interaction radius; clear-block range is intentionally separate. Stand enumeration includes diagonals and lower/upper slope cells throughout the real three-block use radius. When no exact path currently reaches one, setup retains the original fence/gate/water request plus a target-adjacent placement-capable final stand. The shared return helper converges on that same stand through the gate, may safely clear its route or yield to bounded pillar recovery, and cools the placement only after the bounded episode fails. If terrain hides every final stand from the current region, one bounded claim-entry recovery runs before final-stand resolution. Generic perimeter staging is not recursively reselected.

The gate is the omitted perimeter fence cell, never a crop/path cell. Its facing points from the outside toward the interior, giving the correct axis and connections to both required lateral fence-line neighbors. Saved invalid gate coordinates migrate to that opening; misplaced owned gates are reclaimed, and wrong-facing gates at the opening are repaired in place. Fence, gate, water, till, crop planting, bone meal, builder-block, and farm-torch delays are silent: a successful mutation alone produces one renderer-appropriate main-hand action, using either the normal NPC swing or the dedicated non-repeating Epic Fight use animation.

Older unfinished plans that predate entrance validation are recovered rather than immediately discarded. Setup first clears a validated nearby route obstruction, then runs a bounded return-to-claim episode using shared local navigation, route clearing, and pillar recovery. An entrance meaningfully above the NPC can request the shared forced upward goal immediately; a same/lower route that remains blocked for a full return episode requests a short local rise before retrying. Only completed failed recovery episodes count toward replacing a still-unbuilt plan. A farm with irrigation, perimeter, tilled soil, crops, or completed state keeps its ownership.

If no plan exists and the NPC is stranded on elevated gathering terrain, farm-area exploration is allowed to request the shared bounded exploration climb. This prevents strict reachable-site validation from leaving the worker idle on a ledge while still keeping new claims local and reachable.

Every climb request created by `ExploreAroundGoal` records the issuing exploration mode and target. If the escape episode abandons that target or its request expires without reaching a safe surface, only that same exploration mode may consume the handoff. It chooses a bounded exact-reachable local stand away from the failed target and walks there even while the short hole cooldown is active; unrelated exploration modes and non-exploration idle goals cannot steal the fallback.

An upward route request whose destination is outside the claimed farm is intercepted before ordinary pillar planning. The request is the authorization to leave: merely standing inside an intentional fence enclosure is never treated as a hole and cannot start gate egress. The escape goal retains a bounded gate-egress episode across preemption, opens the saved gate, preserves the original forced/exploration request kind for fallback, and keeps the interior/exterior destination separate from any temporary navigation waypoint. If an exact route cannot be created or an accepted route makes no progress for one second, it scans a bounded three-wide, two-block-high corridor from the current feet to the desired waypoint and uses `ClearBlockAi` on the actual reachable obstruction before considering a strictly closer intermediate farm-path cell. Requested and ray-retargeted blocks are checked before and after every clear tick, while failed clear targets receive a bounded cooldown; farm ground/support, current feet/support, farmland, water, crops, expected perimeter, home/build blocks, block entities, and temporary infrastructure stay protected. Three failed episodes restore the original climb once through an anti-reentry bypass; generic pillar bases and route clearing still reject the farm, preventing the old farmland-clear loop and interior/exterior waypoint flip-flop.

Crop operations use strict readiness rather than the saved `READY` phase alone. Irrigation, fence/gate, healthy path support, and farmland under every exposed crop cell must all be valid. A crop already occupying a cell is preserved until its normal harvest; once that cell is exposed, dirt/grass or repaired support rewinds the persisted phase to `TILL`. FarmCrop start, continuation, and supply exploration all yield while this setup work is pending, so FarmSetup crafts/equips the hoe, reaches the exact stand, tills once, and only then releases planting.
